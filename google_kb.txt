Q: What is Gemini by Google?
A: Gemini is Google's next-generation AI assistant, designed to provide advanced conversational capabilities across various Google services. It leverages multimodal large language models to understand and generate text, code, and more, enhancing productivity and user interaction.

Q: How does Gemini differ from Google Assistant?
A: While Google Assistant handles straightforward tasks like setting reminders or playing music, Gemini offers more advanced features, including code generation, document summarization, and deeper integration with Google Workspace apps.
